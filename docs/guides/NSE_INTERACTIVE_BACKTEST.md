# Interactive NSE backtesting

The NSE workspace now mirrors the crypto simulator: settings on the left, results
on the right, numbered configure/review sections, compact summary cards, matching
equity/candlestick charts and result tabs. Data explorer and saved research are
preserved below in a collapsed section. Narrow screens stack the two columns.

Completed results appear immediately. **Play replay** starts from the beginning;
**Pause**, **Next bar**, **Reset replay** and speed choices let you inspect each
stage without rerunning the strategy. Reached candles, equity, fills and charges
are revealed only at their original clocks. A next-opening fill at the same
timestamp as the current completed close remains hidden until the next bar.
Daily outcomes show only completed sessions; partial CSVs contain only reached
fills. Full JSON/cost comparison appears at replay end. This is playback of a
completed backtest, not a live market feed or a new research evaluation.

Open http://localhost:8501/?page=equities. At the top, select minute/hourly data,
a screened company, strategy, lookback, dates, starting INR capital and target
allocation, then **Run NSE backtest**. Runs are explicit; changing controls does
not rerun or relabel previous results. Optional doubled costs use the same signals
but independent funded accounts. A maximum31 calendar days keeps interactive runs
bounded. Dates are restricted to the existing Jan9–Jun30,2026 development window.

Three long/flat templates: trend following, mean reversion, and long during the
session. Trailing closes reset each session; they cannot borrow prices across a
session boundary. Decisions execute at next observed opening through the existing
NSE strategy/risk/OMS/venue/ledger. Existing final-opening liquidation remains;
every session must finish flat. Cash carries exactly into the next replayed
session, without daily capital reset or a fabricated terminal exit.

Minute uses the verified repaired v2 data and existing brokerage/tax reference
charges plus5bps slippage per side. Hourly uses original verified source adapter
and existing15bps fee +5bps slippage proxies. Quarantined share units are blocked.
Only complete screened sessions replay; excluded expected sessions and their
reasons remain visible and returns stay unknown. This is retrospective conditional
development replay, not fresh confirmation, eligibility, or evidence of executable
liquidity. No price gap filling, promotion, research-registry writes or TEST access.

Results include exact INR equity/P&L, drawdown, simulated orders/fills, cash fees,
slippage, source prices with fill markers, trade CSV, session/exclusion tables,
cost comparison and JSON download. V2 session-level fee adjustments are shown
separately; their costs ARE included in total cash fees even when fill.fee is0.

Each run is exclusive under ignored reports_out/nse_sandbox/nse_ui_UUID. Config,
code commit/dirty state, full Python source archive, source/input hashes, selected
observations and signals are frozen before P&L. Final result and artifact hashes
are immutable; failed replays retain a failure receipt. Changed source/data during
execution or tampered saved artifacts fail instead of displaying trusted metrics.
These sandbox artifacts do not become formal research results or Alpha tracker
entries merely because a user runs them repeatedly. They do not route real orders.

Synthetic data is generated by software to resemble a market, such as a scripted
trend, range or crash. Build & simulate uses generated BTCUSDT prices to test
software behavior. NSE backtest instead uses retained vendor observations and
simulates trades against those historical bars; the two sources remain labelled.
